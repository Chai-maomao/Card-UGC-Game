CARDEX 创作与分享规范 / UGC guidelines

创作自己的名称、技能说明和插画；使用别人的素材前核对授权与署名条件。不要假冒官方或其他作者。不得传播侵权、违法、仇恨、骚扰、色情剥削、恶意文件或未经允许的个人信息。

导入前检查卡牌内容、技能复杂度与来源；规则预算会限制极端内容，但不保证任何 UGC 平衡。不要把自制内容宣传为官方竞技认证。分享文件和对局可能包含完整卡牌信息与插画；不要把密码、联系方式或定位写入卡图/卡名。

本版本没有公共内容审核平台；直连/房间中玩家互相发送内容。遇到问题可停止导入、删除本地副本、离开房间并向服务器运营者或项目 Issues 报告。投诉请提供内容描述和权利依据，避免公开敏感资料。维护者不能远程删除别人设备上的副本。

Create original names, skill text and art; check rights and attribution before reusing others' work. Do not impersonate creators or share unlawful, infringing, hateful, harassing, exploitative, malicious or private content. Inspect imports and origins. Complexity budgets do not guarantee balance or official competitive approval. Shared files/matches can expose full card data and images.
There is no public moderation platform in this desktop build. Players exchange content through rooms. Stop importing, remove local copies, leave the room and report issues to the operator or linked project tracker. Provide a description and rights basis without exposing private data. Maintainers cannot remotely delete copies on other devices.
